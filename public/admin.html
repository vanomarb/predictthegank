Log it straight

Every prediction on this page is built from what people log. Random or joke entries do not just add noise — they move the predicted times for everyone.

  • Only log a roam you actually saw. Not one you expect, and not one you heard about.
  • Log it as it happens. The minute is the whole point — catching up an hour later teaches the pattern the wrong time.
  • Once per roam. A teammate may have logged it seconds ago; the app merges those, but only if they are close together.
